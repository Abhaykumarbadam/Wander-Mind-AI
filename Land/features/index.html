Features

Adaptive Itineraries

WanderMind dynamically adjusts day-wise plans to weather, unexpected closures and your preferences. It proposes alternative activities, local shortcuts, and estimated durations with clear reasons — so you can decide quickly and travel confidently.

Conversational Planning

Chat naturally to refine your trip: ask for food-friendly routes, slow or fast days, and cultural notes. The assistant supports follow-ups, offers concise tradeoffs, and summarizes changes so your plan stays interpretable and trustworthy.

Smart Maps & Visualization

Visual timelines, pin-based stops, and route overlays create a clear travel flow. Hover pins for short summaries and time estimates, or expand to see reviews and directions — everything visual and actionable.

Seamless Transport & Stay

WanderMind surfaces curated transport and accommodation options sorted by budget and convenience — plus timing-aware transfer suggestions, so transitions between activities remain smooth and stress-free.
